02 Identity Concept

Local Art. Surprising Places.

The Boulder Flipside visual identity is a system made to speak boldly and read clearly.

The brand stands out on the street, sticks in people’s minds, and welcomes every part of Boulder’s art scene.

The look comes from printmaking, where every stamp, block, and screen prints the mirror image of what’s carved into it.

02 Identity Primary logotype

The brand name is set in a customized typeface with the tactile edge of a stamp that’s simple enough to read against busy artwork. Inspired by hand-set lettering, the words overlap. One letter flips in an homage to the concept, without making the name difficult to read. Boulder is weighted equally to Flipside.

02 Identity Brand mark

An outline of the B with the F inside, so the mark makes its own holding shape.

The brand mark replaces the logo anywhere the name would be too small to read. It’s also used for digital applications like social media avatars and the website favicon.

02 Identity Color

The colors take after ink on a hand-printed poster, where every print comes out a little different. Bright and fresh, they make each piece look like it just came off the press.

02 Identity Type

Large headlines are set in Canteen BN.

Smaller details and longer blocks of text are set in PP Fraktion Mono.
