02

Logo use

Clear spaceHalf the logo’s height on every side
Logo minimum size1 in or 120px wide
Brand mark minimum size0.25 in or 16px wide

If the logo would run smaller than its minimum size, use the brand mark.

Avoid

Keep the logo as drawn. Don’t stretch or squeeze it, set it in any color but black or white, move or resize the words, or add shadows or outlines.

04

Composition

Layout

Every layout is built on the same grid. Text, imagery, and logos line up to it. The dashed line marks the safe area. Keep everything inside it.

Margin
6–10% of the layout’s shorter side, the same on every side.
Gutter
1% of the layout’s shorter side.
Grid
12 columns and 12 rows inside the margin.
Safe area
The margin box.
Images
An 8px rounded edge.
Layers

Every layout starts with a color. Textures go on top of it, then the text, the imagery, and the logos.
