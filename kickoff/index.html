03

Scope of work

The phases run in the order below. Every phase ends with a review meeting. I bring finished work, we go through it together, and then we move to the next phase. Small edits are handled inline and shown within the next presentation or deliverable.

01Kickoff

We review the scope of work and build the inputs that shape the brand decisions.

Includes
  • Kickoff meeting. With everyone on your project team, covering:
    • The scope of work, the schedule, and how we work together
    • The creative brief and materials your team provided
    • Creative direction. Who the program is for, the goal for visual communication, and directions to avoid
You get
  • Project brief. A written summary of the kickoff, with the approach, schedule, and deliverables confirmed. Sent before the brand character meeting.
02Brand character

Two conceptual directions that preview the brand look and feel.

Includes
  • Brand character previews. Two, developed in parallel, each with:
    • Logo mark directions to guide the design of your brand symbol
    • Layout and typography ideas to set the tone for your system
    • Samples of visual elements like photography, illustration, and graphics that express your story
    • A suggested color palette to anchor your look and feel
  • Review meeting. We look at both directions together, and you pick the one that moves forward.
You get
  • Brand character document. Each direction, with the thinking behind it.
  • Selected direction. Carried into Brand identity.
03Brand identity and campaign materials

The marks for your brand, built out across the pieces the program needs for the announcement and the festival. Everything is presented together in one review meeting.

Includes
  • Primary mark. With a logo lockup and secondary iteration.
  • Iconic mark. A simplified graphic for the social avatar, the favicon, and other small applications.
  • Application design. The identity across social, print, and the storefronts.
  • Review meeting. The identity and every application, shown together with the thinking behind them.
You get
  • Brand identity document. With the reasoning behind the choices.
  • Logo artwork. Every mark and arrangement, in AI, EPS, PDF, PNG, and SVG.
  • Social templates. Two, one with image and text and one text only.
  • CRM templates. Two, square, in the same two arrangements.
  • Print poster template. One, built around the copy your team provides.
  • Window application. One design for the storefront glass.
  • Signage and wayfinding concept. One design for a second storefront placement.
  • Figma file. With the social and CRM templates.
  • Brand guidelines. A page with the logo, color, type, graphic elements, and the rules for using them.
