A Recap Brand character
Progressive

Bold and contemporary, closer to the art you’d find in a city like New York, LA, or London.

Word mark

Boulder stays as prominent as Flipside in every lockup.

Type first

The identity is built around the name, with room for negative space, mirroring, rotation, and flipped letterforms.

Wayfinding

Obvious from the street, and clear that everyone’s welcome to walk in.

Every kind of program

Visual art, sound, performance, immersive work, talks. No reference to any one medium.

A Recap Directions to avoid
Generic Colorado

Mountain triangles, the Colorado flag and its colors, ski and climbing gear, aspen leaves, natural greens and browns.

Flatirons

Left out, or a subtle reference within the overall identity.

Sundance

Film imagery, the rounded 16:9 frame, the yellow circle, primary-color gradients.

Nostalgia

Retro and vintage looks.

Brand character

Pressed by hand.

A tactile identity inspired by printmaking and the name itself. Bright colors overlap, and free-form layouts bring energy to every piece.
