A clear, bold identity made to invite people in. The flip is built into the letters of the name, and the longer someone looks, the more they find. It’s made to be spotted from the street and to reward a second look.

04 Direction 02 Overview

A hands-on identity that feels made by the artists it celebrates. The flip comes from printmaking, where every print is the mirror of the stamp or screen that made it. It pairs the energy of a big-city art opening with the craft of local makers.

Feel

Hands-on, layered, off-register

In the spirit of

The energy of an opening in an unexpected space, where the work feels close enough to touch.
