Direction 01

Look again.

A modern identity inspired by contemporary art galleries, with neon-bright color and a clean, commanding look that pulls people in from the street.

02 Direction 01 Vibe board

This direction takes the look of a contemporary art gallery and brings it out to the sidewalk. Clean layouts and bold type give it the confidence of a museum, and neon-bright color signals from the street that art is open inside. The flip is built into the letters of the name, so the longer someone looks, the more they find.

Direction 02

Pressed by hand.

An identity inspired by printmaking, where every stamp is the mirror image of what it prints, so the program feels made by the artists it celebrates.

03 Direction 02 Vibe board

This direction takes its flip from printmaking, where every stamp, block, and screen is the mirror image of what it prints. Layered ink, bright color, and lettering with a handmade edge make the program feel made by the artists it celebrates. It pairs the energy of a big-city art opening with the craft of local makers.
