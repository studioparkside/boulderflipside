Direction 01

Look again.

A modern identity inspired by contemporary art galleries, with neon-bright color and a clean, commanding look that pulls people in from the street.

01 Direction 01 Overview

This direction mixes the look of a contemporary art gallery with the energy of the city at night. Clean layouts and bold type give it the confidence of a museum. Neon-bright color signals from the street that art is open inside. The logo and layouts take inspiration from the name, so the longer someone looks, the more they find.

01 Direction 01 Logotype

The name could be set in bold, simple letters that read from across the street. Letters built from basic geometric shapes let us mirror and simplify some of them, so the type itself plays on the name. Tight, bold lettering makes every piece look like part of one identity.

01 Direction 01 Logo mark

The logo mark could pull the B from Boulder and the F from Flipside into one shape. Borrowing from ambigrams, it could be designed to read the same when it’s turned or mirrored.

01 Direction 01 Color

These colors are bold, high contrast, and inspired by neon signs. In a window, on a sidewalk, or on a phone, they signal that art is open inside.

01 Direction 01 Layout

Like a gallery wall, big type and open space turn event details into the design. Some layouts could mirror from one side to the other, giving windows and other two-sided signage a front and a flip side.

Direction 02

Pressed by hand.

A tactile identity inspired by printmaking and the name itself. Bright colors overlap, and free-form layouts bring energy to every piece.

02 Direction 02 Overview

This direction takes the Flipside name to printmaking, where every stamp, block, and screen is the mirror image of what it prints. Layered ink, bright color, and lettering with a handmade edge make the program feel made by the artists we celebrate. The energy of a big-city art opening, paired with the craft of local makers, gives it a distinctly Boulder feel.

02 Direction 02 Logotype

The name could be set in lettering with the slightly uneven edges you see on stamps, hand-pulled prints, and screen-printed posters, so the word mark feels made by hand. Part of it could even be flipped the way a stamp is before it touches paper.

02 Direction 02 Logo mark

The logo mark could layer the letters of the name over each other, possibly inside a stamp. Like a stamp at the door, it gives us an easy way to mark each venue as part of the program.

02 Direction 02 Color

Bright inks overlap into new colors, the way they do on a print. A wide range of colors lets the brand own the whole palette.

02 Direction 02 Layout

Expressive type and collaged layouts make each piece feel like an object someone made on purpose. Some pieces could turn design elements on their side or run text upside down, so the poster turns in your hands.
